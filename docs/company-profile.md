## 2. Origin story

The AI Heroes brand and track record date back to 2019. Frans Hoorn spent three years in management at that original AI Heroes and David Homan two years in operations. At the end of 2025, Frans, Jan Brusse and David acquired the brand and client relationships from its previous owner and relaunched the company; AI Heroes B.V. was incorporated in Groningen on 1 May 2026. Joseph Groot Kormelink, from the earlier era, remains connected as industry advisor. The company is backed by a small pre-seed investment from two Groningen entrepreneurs who act as mentors and sparring partners without an operational role.

The founding observation, unchanged since the relaunch: *"The AI market is fragmented. Advisory firms write reports, training bureaus train and leave, tech companies build tools nobody understands. Nobody covers the full spectrum: from organisational change to technical implementation."* AI Heroes exists to be that one partner.

## 3. Positioning

Four interconnected messaging pillars, used consistently everywhere:

1. **Full-service AI agency** — three disciplines under one roof; each founder is the specialist for one pillar. Differentiator script: "We dekken het hele spectrum. Geen split tussen advies en tech. Het team dat je roadmap maakt kan ook bouwen wat erin staat."
2. **Van change management tot technische implementatie** — change management is not a separate service; the whole agency is built on the idea that AI success requires organisational change.
3. **Groningen, AI-hoofdstad van Europa / AI Capital of Europe** — stated in the present tense, without hedging. The €200M AI Fabriek (supercomputer, expertise centre) is proof, not a precondition. "Wij zijn de lokale agency die er al zit."
4. **European-first** — data sovereignty, EU AI Act compliance, no vendor lock-in. This is an operating principle, not just marketing: the company runs its own stack on EU infrastructure and is migrating off US processors. Tone note: the EU angle is a differentiator, deliberately not an ultimatum — clients happy on Copilot/US stacks are served just as well.

**Approved phrases:** "Eén agency voor het hele AI-traject" · "Van directiekamer tot werkvloer" · "Praktijk gaat voor theorie" · "Als AI niet de oplossing is, zeggen we dat" · "Waar je ook staat met AI, we kunnen helpen" · "Alles van A tot I" · "Drie pijlers, één partner".

**Avoid:** "AI consultancy" as company descriptor · "AI journey" · NL-only framing · negative contrasts ("geen X, maar Y") · em-dashes · hype and LinkedIn-guru cadence.

## 4. Values

Public values (the "Wat ons drijft" set):

1. **Eerlijkheid boven verkoop / Honesty over sales** — "If AI isn't the solution, we'll tell you." Backed by real guarantees: the business-case analysis is free if nothing valuable is found, and "no vendor lock-in" is a written promise.
2. **Maatwerk boven standaard / Custom over standard.**
3. **Practitioners die meedoen / Practitioners who do the work** — "We don't just advise, we also build."
4. **Groningen, AI Capital of Europe** — local base, European reach.

Operating principles visible in how the company actually runs:

- **Radical dogfooding** — internal busywork is automated with self-built AI tools (meeting transcription and task extraction, report generation, slide decks, dashboards): "anders zouden we geen AI'ers zijn."
- **Speed as a sales weapon** — first to respond wins; 24-hour contact promise; 6-day prototype sprints.
- **Practice over theory, results over reports** — every engagement aims at something that works, not a document.
- **Learn from everyone** — including deliberately talking to competitors to learn, not to poach.
- **Frugality** — runway discipline over startup glamour.
- **Honest, direct culture** — flat organisation, trust-based agreements, "geen snelle jongens."

## 6. Ideal client profile (two-tier)

### Tier 1 — core: Dutch MKB starting or structuring their AI adoption
- **Who:** owner-led and MT-led SMEs, roughly 10–350 FTE, with a strong base in Noord-Nederland; decision-maker is the owner, director or MT, often supported by HR/IT/privacy officers.
- **Sectors seen in practice:** healthcare and care organisations, agri and machinery, construction/demolition, logistics, retail and eCommerce, professional services, sports/leisure.
- **Maturity:** typically level 2 of the AI Heroes five-level MKB model (Niets → Proberen → Afspraken → Ingebouwd → Vliegwiel) — individuals experimenting, no agreements or structure yet.
- **Pain profile:** information scattered across WhatsApp/Excel/paper, heavy manual admin (quotes, work plans, lookup work), staff already experimenting with AI without policy.
- **Buying triggers:** EU AI Act Article 4 AI-literacy obligation (enforcement in NL from 2 Aug 2026), talent shortage, measurable admin burden, regional subsidies (MIT Noord-NL up to €50–100k can cover a large share of a project).
- **How they arrive:** referrals and network (ecosystem partners, existing clients, personal networks), workshops as the entry product, then scan → roadmap → build.

### Tier 2 — halo: enterprise, government, education and EU-driven organisations
- Enterprise and government **training** engagements (Tweede Kamer, Philips Healthcare, Prosus, Nationale Postcode Loterij, Banijay Benelux, Envalior).
- **Education**: Hanzehogeschool, RUG ecosystem; discounted rates for schools and non-profits.
- **International product work** through relationships (OLX Poland, iFood Brazil, InnoEnergy).
- Organisations for whom **EU data sovereignty is a hard requirement** — the EU-consultancy/EU-development propositions.

These names double as proof for Tier 1: "van startups tot Fortune 500, van gemeenten tot media-organisaties" is the range claim; the MKB is where the day-to-day growth happens.

**Anti-ICP / walk-away rules:** if AI is not the answer, say so and decline the work; no unpaid "maybe" projects — clear go/no-go moments; no clients who expect a report instead of a result.

## 9. Brand voice and channels

- **Register:** observing, analytical, dry where possible, sharp where needed. Benchmark: write like Tweakers/NU.nl, never like marketing copy or LinkedIn gurus. Lay facts side by side; let the reader conclude.
- **Hard rules:** no em-dashes; no "niet X, maar Y" constructions; no moralistic endings; four approved ending types (pragmatic, observational, analytical, warning); full organisation names on first mention; double quotes only for literal citations.
- **Channel:** LinkedIn is the channel — 2–3 posts/week, rotating between the three founders. Dutch for the company voice; English only for AI Salon content. Recurring formats: news-analysis opinion posts, "Wist je dat…"-explainers ('Wat is'-series: RAG, MCP, agents, prompt injection…), service cards, case posts, team profiles.
- **Visual identity:** geometric/square-frame motif; four colors only — Foundry Red #D9534F (Training), Catalyst Blue #2563EB (Consulting), Forged Black #1C1917 (Software), Rooftop White #FDFCF8; Playfair Display headings, Inter body. Banner tagline: "AI strategie. Implementatie. Training."
- **Audience of the content:** non-technical MKB decision-makers — "AI begrijpelijk maken voor mensen die er geen techneut voor willen worden."

## 11. Market thesis and ambitions

**Why now (the market case):** the Dutch AI market is ~€7.4B (Statista 2025) and growing 20%+ per year; 95% of Dutch organisations run an AI programme; the EU AI Act's Article 4 AI-literacy obligation creates structural training demand from August 2026; the gap between AI ambition and execution keeps widening while talent stays scarce.

**Direction (2026–2029):**
- Grow the MKB core through the network flywheel: workshops as entry, scan → roadmap → build as the path, subsidies lowering the threshold.
- Build the **Groningen ecosystem position**: AI Salon as community flagship, visible partner of AI Fabriek/RUG/Hanze, "the local agency that's already there" when the AI capital claim materialises.
- Scale **training nationally** through the ZZP trainer network; develop a "Community of Learners" concept for recurring advanced trainings (agentic engineering, Claude Code, open-source models).
- Build out the **compliance proposition**: first get AI Heroes itself certified for ISO 42001 (AI management) and ISO 27001 (information security), then help clients implement those standards — turning the company's own certification journey into a repeatable service. This also supports **government and tender work** as a deliberate long game.
- Incubate **product bets** alongside services (R&D under WBSO): Tender Helper (AI tender-response assistant, public launch targeted at AI Salon on 3 September 2026), EU-hosted AI offerings, plus internal tools (transcription/task extraction, LLM-output evaluation) that may mature into products.
- Deepen the **EU-sovereignty proposition** as more organisations demand European infrastructure — offered as an option, never an ultimatum.
