## The question

Administration that passes through hands costs time that could have gone to customers. Kwakkel BV wanted to know whether that work could be automated, and did not want to take a report's word for it. They wanted to see it.

<div class="bg-stone-50 p-6 md:p-8 mb-8 border-l-4 border-brand-red not-prose">
  <h3 class="text-xl font-serif text-brand-dark mb-3">What was there after six days</h3>
  <ul class="space-y-2 text-stone-600 list-disc ml-5">
    <li><strong>A working prototype</strong> that processes administrative steps automatically, on Kwakkel BV's own data</li>
    <li><strong>A demo</strong> for the people who do the work today, so they could say what was still missing</li>
    <li><strong>A business case</strong> on one page: what a full build costs, what it returns and when it pays back</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## How we approached it

We started with the people who do the administration, not with the technology. Which steps come back every day, where it goes wrong, what data already exists. Then we built the prototype in a six-day sprint, connected it to the real data and let it run alongside the current work.

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 not-prose">
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">On real data</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">No demo with invented examples. From day four the prototype processed Kwakkel BV's actual administration.</p></div>
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">Human in the loop</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">Every automated step can be traced and corrected. What the system is not sure about, it hands to an employee.</p></div>
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">Business case first</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">Before any build decision, Kwakkel BV knew what the full version costs and what it returns.</p></div>
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">Owned by Kwakkel BV</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">Source code, documentation and data model of the prototype belong to the client, even if the build does not go ahead.</p></div>
</div>
