## The question

INQ22 wanted its own web shop: not a standard package with a theme on top, but a shop that does exactly what the brand needs and that belongs to INQ22. The question was one we hear often: "Can you build this for us?"

<div class="bg-stone-50 p-6 md:p-8 mb-8 border-l-4 border-brand-blue not-prose">
  <h3 class="text-xl font-serif text-brand-dark mb-3">Why this is an AI Heroes case</h3>
  <ul class="space-y-2 text-stone-600 list-disc ml-5">
    <li><strong>There is no AI in the shop.</strong> The software is the product, and that is exactly what we mean by "software and AI": AI is our tool, not our goal</li>
    <li><strong>Built with AI.</strong> The team works with AI tooling in design, code and test; that is what makes a custom shop affordable for an organisation of this size</li>
    <li><strong>Live.</strong> The shop runs on <a href="https://inq22.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">inq22.com</a>; you can see it for yourself</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## What we built

A custom web shop: catalogue, basket, checkout, back office. Designed around the brand and the way INQ22 sells, connected to payment and shipping, and handed over with documentation so INQ22 manages the shop itself.

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 not-prose">
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">Owned by INQ22</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">Source code, design and data belong to INQ22. No subscription to a platform that sets the rules.</p></div>
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">Handed over</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">The INQ22 team was trained on managing the shop. Products, prices and copy they manage themselves.</p></div>
</div>

A web shop that runs live and belongs to the client. See for yourself at <a href="https://inq22.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">inq22.com</a>.
