## The question

If you advise organisations to keep AI in Europe, you have to do it yourself. The assistant in the bottom right of this site is our answer: an AI assistant that answers questions about AI Heroes, built by the current team, on infrastructure under European law.

<div class="bg-stone-50 p-6 md:p-8 mb-8 border-l-4 border-brand-blue not-prose">
  <h3 class="text-xl font-serif text-brand-dark mb-3">What is in it</h3>
  <ul class="space-y-2 text-stone-600 list-disc ml-5">
    <li><strong>European hosting:</strong> the functions run in Frankfurt, the database sits with a European region</li>
    <li><strong>Public sources only:</strong> the assistant answers from a search index over the public pages of this site, and says so when it does not know</li>
    <li><strong>Transparency under the AI Act:</strong> every visitor sees in the conversation itself that it is an AI assistant, with a page explaining how it works</li>
    <li><strong>Retention policy:</strong> conversations are deleted automatically after a fixed period</li>
    <li><strong>A human within reach:</strong> anyone who wants a person gets the conversation forwarded to the team, with a reply by email</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## How we built it

The way we do it for clients. First the question: what must the assistant be able to do, and above all what not. Then a prototype, tested with real visitor questions. Then compliance in the design: where the data lives, who can reach it, how long we keep it, how a visitor knows they are talking to AI. Only then live, with a spending cap, a rate limiter and logging of what goes wrong.

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 not-prose">
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">No made-up answers</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">The assistant cites pages on this site and quotes no prices that are not on it. If it does not know, it says so and offers a human.</p></div>

  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">Tested before go-live</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">A fixed set of test conversations has to pass before a new version may go live. That is how the assistant keeps doing what it should.</p></div>
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">Publicly documented</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">How the assistant works, what it keeps and for how long, is on its own page on this site.</p></div>
