## The question

IC Commerce runs an e-commerce platform where a lot of work still passed through hands: updating product data, writing copy, processing orders and questions. The platform itself did not need to go; the work around it had to change.

<div class="bg-stone-50 p-6 md:p-8 mb-8 border-l-4 border-brand-red not-prose">
  <h3 class="text-xl font-serif text-brand-dark mb-3">What it was about</h3>
  <ul class="space-y-2 text-stone-600 list-disc ml-5">
    <li><strong>Manual work:</strong> the same steps, every day, for every product and every order</li>
    <li><strong>Growth:</strong> more products and more customers without proportionally more people</li>
    <li><strong>Knowledge:</strong> a partner that knows the market and gets to know the business</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## How we approached it

Step by step. We went through the process from product data to customer contact, picked the step where automation returned the most, built it, and moved on to the next. Each step first ran alongside the manual work, with an employee watching, before it replaced it.

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8 not-prose">
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">Step by step</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">No platform replaced; the work around it automated, one step at a time, in the order of what returned the most.</p></div>
  <div class="p-6 bg-white border border-stone-200"><h4 class="font-bold text-brand-dark mb-2">Human in the loop</h4><p class="text-sm text-stone-600">Every automated step can be checked and corrected. What the system is not sure about goes to an employee.</p></div>
</div>
