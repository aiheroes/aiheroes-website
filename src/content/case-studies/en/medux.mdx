## For clients who would rather call

Many of Medux's clients prefer to arrange things by phone. As the number of clients grew, customer service became harder to scale. So we built an AI voice for the routine phone work, with handover to a staff member for difficult and urgent questions.

## The figures

4,000 calls a month, 80% of them handled by AI. A 70% cost saving and a 7.8 rating for the AI conversations. The figures were measured by Medux.

## About Medux

Medux is a Dutch healthcare organisation that supplies assistive devices and care technology, including for people with reduced mobility.
