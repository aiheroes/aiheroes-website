## Software that follows the work

The subscription had to be paid every year, for features that mostly went unused, and the package dictated how Strive had to work. The new software does only what the gym needs, connected to what was already there.

Because the new system was built alongside the package, work at the gym simply carried on. The replacement paid for itself in one year.
