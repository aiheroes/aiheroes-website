## A prototype to decide on

With that many portfolio companies, it quickly becomes hard to keep track of growth, funding and risks. The prototype gave InnoEnergy's board a concrete picture of the platform, and the idea won the board's support, with a roadmap as the next step.

## About InnoEnergy

InnoEnergy is a European investor in sustainable energy, with more than 200 portfolio companies and more than 1,200 partners.
