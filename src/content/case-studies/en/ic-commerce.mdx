## Step by step

IC Commerce wanted to handle more products and more customers without a matching number of extra people. First came a platform of its own in place of the WooCommerce shop. Then we went through the work around it, from product data to customer contact, and automated the steps one by one, starting with the one that delivered the most.

Whatever the system is unsure about, it puts to a staff member. That way every automated step can be checked and corrected.
