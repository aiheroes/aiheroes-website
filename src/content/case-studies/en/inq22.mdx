## Made for studios

INQ22 sells to professionals, and the shop is set up for that: search by item number, quick reordering and prices the way a business customer expects them. The INQ22 team manages products, prices and copy themselves.

## Software without AI, built with AI

The shop itself contains no AI. We did build it with AI as a tool, in design, code and testing. INQ22 shows that we build what a client needs, with or without AI in the product.
