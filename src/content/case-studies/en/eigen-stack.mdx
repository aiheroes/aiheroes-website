## Try it yourself

The assistant sits at the bottom right of every page. When it does not know something, it says so and offers you a person. Conversations are deleted automatically after a fixed period. How it works, what it stores and for how long is on [the page about the AI assistant](/en/legal/ai-assistant).

## Why this is a case

We advise organisations to keep AI in Europe, so we do the same. Here we answered for ourselves the questions we ask clients about hosting, access, retention and transparency.
