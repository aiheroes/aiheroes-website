## What Kwakkel had after six days

A working prototype, a demo for the staff and a one-page business case: what the full build costs, what it returns and when it pays for itself. With those figures, Kwakkel decides for itself whether and when the build starts.

Kwakkel BV was the first organisation where the current team worked this way. It is the approach we have called [the start sprint](/en/start-sprint) ever since.
