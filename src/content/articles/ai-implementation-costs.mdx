## Where the money goes

### Software: EUR 15,000 to 100,000 and beyond

<a href="/en/services/software#custom-ai-solutions">Custom software and AI</a> has the widest spread. A well-scoped application, like automating one document flow, sits at the lower end. Systems with multiple integrations, high accuracy requirements or heavy compliance sit at the top; the same goes for replacing software that no longer fits and for system integration. We always start with a <a href="/en/start-sprint">start sprint</a>: one week, from EUR 8,000, resulting in a working prototype or build plan plus business case (at <a href="/en/cases/trabu">Trabu</a> the prototype stood in 6 days). That start sprint is the proof of concept; only then does the full build begin. Business case, compliance, training of the team and handover without lock-in are included in every build.

### Consulting: EUR 3,000 to 50,000 and beyond

At the lower end sits the <a href="/en/services/consulting#ai-readiness-scan">AI readiness assessment</a> (from EUR 3,000): in 1 to 3 days you know where you stand and what the logical next step is. With us the <a href="/en/services/consulting#ai-roadmap">AI roadmap</a> is a phase of a build; only the start sprint carries a fixed price, from EUR 8,000. Rule of thumb: the bigger the decision that follows, the more the groundwork may cost.

### Training: EUR 2,500 to 4,000 per day

<a href="/en/services/incompany-ai-training">In-company AI training</a> is the cheapest investment with the broadest effect: your whole team works faster and more safely with AI afterwards. The price varies with group size and customisation. In a build, training of the team is included; it is also available on its own. Compare that with the hidden cost of not training: employees reinventing every wheel, or pasting company data into public tools.

## The costs nobody budgets

Three items that are often missing from quotes but decide the business case:

- **Data preparation.** If your data is scattered, polluted or unstructured, time goes there before AI can do anything with it.
- **Adoption.** A working system nobody uses returns nothing. Reserve budget for training and change management.
- **Maintenance.** Models, integrations and regulation keep changing. Count on an annual amount for upkeep and iteration.

## What does it return?

Costs without returns mean nothing. At <a href="/en/cases/medux">Medux</a>, a custom AI solution delivered a 70% cost reduction on a labour-intensive process. Not every project hits numbers like that; that is why we quantify the business case before building, and sometimes advise not to build at all.
