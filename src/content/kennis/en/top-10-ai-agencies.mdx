## How this list was made

Search for "AI agency Netherlands" or "AI consultancy Netherlands" and you mostly find lists full of foreign names or thinly disguised adverts. This is our version, and we are open about how it came about:

- **AI Heroes is number 1, because this is our list.** We are not impartial about ourselves.
- **We describe the other agencies based on what they publish themselves:** their services, cases and location, checked on their own websites in October 2026.
- **Places 2 to 10 are not a quality ranking.** Each agency is strong in something different; the guide at the bottom helps you choose.
- **We review the list every six months.** The date at the top shows when that last happened. If something is wrong, let us know.

## The criteria

1. **Based in the Netherlands**, with a team that works here.
2. **Verifiable work:** named cases or publicly visible projects.
3. **What the agency delivers:** building, data engineering, training or advice.
4. **Specialism:** what the agency excels at, and therefore who it makes most sense for.

## The list

### 1. AI Heroes (Groningen)

- **Strong in:** building AI applications teams use every day, from new applications to replacing old software and running AI on your own servers.
- **Suits:** organisations with a concrete process, system or idea and someone who can decide.
- **Way of working:** always a six-day start sprint at a fixed price first. Business case, compliance and team training are part of every build.
- **Less obvious for:** anyone who only wants an advisory report.

### 2. DataNorth AI (Groningen)

- **Strong in:** a broad offer, from trainings, workshops and demonstrations to advice and development.
- **Suits:** organisations that want to explore AI and bring their people along, with the option of development afterwards.
- **Notable:** also has an office in Amsterdam, and is one of the few agencies that publishes its rates.

### 3. Xomnia (Amsterdam)

- **Strong in:** data science, machine learning engineering, MLOps and generative and agent-based AI. Active since 2013.
- **Suits:** larger organisations that want to set up data and AI structurally and are looking for a steady team of specialists.

### 4. Xebia (Hilversum)

- **Strong in:** data and AI as part of a large IT firm, with strategy, machine learning, MLOps and its own training offer. The former GoDataDriven has become part of Xebia.
- **Suits:** organisations that want to combine AI with cloud, software development and training at one party.

### 5. Rewire (Amsterdam)

- **Strong in:** data and AI for large companies, from strategy and MLOps to solutions for areas such as pricing, supply chain and sales. With its own training academy.
- **Suits:** large organisations with a clear commercial or operational question they want to solve with data.

### 6. BigData Republic (Utrecht)

- **Strong in:** data platforms, predictive models and data infrastructure, with a team of experienced data consultants.
- **Suits:** data-intensive organisations that want their platform and models production-ready.

### 7. Eraneos (Amsterdam and Groningen)

- **Strong in:** management and technology consulting with a Data & AI practice. The AI agency DEUS became part of Eraneos in 2026.
- **Suits:** large organisations that make AI part of a wider transformation programme.

### 8. ai.nl (Utrecht)

- **Strong in:** keynotes, practical training in tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot, and support with strategy and implementation.
- **Suits:** organisations that want to inspire and train their people quickly.

### 9. Enjins (Utrecht and Amsterdam)

- **Strong in:** machine learning applications, from audit and infrastructure to deployment and MLOps.
- **Suits:** scale-ups and mid-sized companies that want to build an ML product or make one production-ready.

### 10. Cmotions (nationwide)

- **Strong in:** the whole data value chain: data strategy, data engineering, data management, analytics and data science, with its own academy.
- **Suits:** organisations that want to get their data in order as a basis for AI, often in a Microsoft environment.
