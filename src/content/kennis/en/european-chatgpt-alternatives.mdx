## How this list was made

- **Criteria:** a European provider or European hosting, clarity about where the data is stored, business terms and what the product can do.
- **Source:** the providers' own websites and documentation, checked in October 2026.
- **Independent:** AI Heroes sells none of these products. We build model-agnostic, so you can switch later.
- **Review:** every six months. Products and names change quickly; Le Chat, for example, has been called Vibe since May 2026.

## European assistants: ready to use

### Mistral Vibe (Mistral AI, France)

- **What it is:** Mistral AI's assistant, known as Le Chat until May 2026. With plans for individuals, teams and organisations.
- **Strong in:** general office work, writing, summarising and searching, with connectors for email and documents, among others.
- **Data:** hosted in the EU by default. Check in your contract which subprocessors handle data.
- **Suits:** organisations that want a full business assistant from a European provider.

### Lumo (Proton, Switzerland)

- **What it is:** the AI assistant from Proton, the company behind Proton Mail, with a business plan.
- **Strong in:** privacy and confidentiality; conversations are not used to train models.
- **Data:** with Proton in Europe. Switzerland is outside the EU, but has its own privacy law that the EU recognises as adequate.
- **Suits:** law firms, financial advisers and other professions where confidentiality comes first.

### Aleph Alpha (Germany)

- **What it is:** a German AI provider focused on governments and large organisations, with a platform for building your own applications.
- **Note:** a combination with the Canadian company Cohere was announced in 2026. Ask what that means for where your data is stored and which law it falls under.
- **Suits:** governments and regulated sectors looking for a European platform for their own applications.

## Open models in a European cloud

If you want to build your own application, such as a knowledge assistant or customer service, you usually use a model through a cloud provider. These European providers offer open models, including Mistral's:

| Provider | Country | What they offer |
|---|---|---|
| **OVHcloud AI Endpoints** | France | Open models through an API, hosted under European jurisdiction |
| **Scaleway Generative APIs** | France | Open models through an API, hosted in Paris |
| **IONOS AI Model Hub** | Germany | Open models through an API, processing in Germany |

**EuroLLM** deserves a separate mention: a fully open language model, trained in Europe for all 24 official EU languages. The largest version, EuroLLM-22B, came out in December 2025. You can run it yourself or use it through a cloud provider.

## On your own servers

For the most sensitive data you run an open model on your own hardware or in your own data centre. Compact open models are good enough for many business tasks, such as summarising, searching documents and processing forms. The data then never leaves your own environment. What that takes and costs is explained in [AI on your own servers](/en/ai-on-your-own-servers).

## The comparison in one table

| Route | Where the data is | Strong in | Suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mistral Vibe | EU, by default | Full business assistant | General office work |
| Lumo | Europe (Switzerland) | Privacy and confidentiality | Professions bound by confidentiality |
| Aleph Alpha | Germany | Platform for your own applications | Government and regulated sectors |
| Open model in a European cloud | France or Germany | Building your own applications | Business processes with customer or company data |
| Open model on your own servers | Your own environment | Full control | The most sensitive data |

## Which one fits?

- **General office work, little sensitive data:** a European assistant such as Mistral Vibe.
- **Confidential files:** an assistant built around privacy, or your own application on a European model.
- **Business processes with customer or company data:** your own application on an open model in a European cloud, connected to your systems.
- **The most sensitive data, such as healthcare or government:** an open model on your own servers.

## The honest caveat

The largest American models are still ahead on some tasks, such as complex reasoning and programming. For most business applications that gap is small. The question is what your application needs. The best way to find out is to test on your own data and questions before you choose.
